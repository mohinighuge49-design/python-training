🎓 About College Smart Portal

Smart Solution for College Information Management

📘 Project Overview

College Smart Portal is a modern web-based application developed using Python, Flask, Bootstrap and Jinja2.

It helps students and staff access college information, student records, notices and statistics through an easy, fast and user-friendly interface.
